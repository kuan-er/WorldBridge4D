## Decision and scope

Reject an immediate mid-run global clip1-to-clip5 switch; continue the main trajectory unchanged at clip1. This is a matched online-training comparison, not fixed held-out evaluation and not evidence about training with clip5 from initialization. If clipping is revisited, prefer a smaller threshold, transition warmup, and/or per-group/adaptive clipping with held-out evaluation.
