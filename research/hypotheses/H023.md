# H023

## Hypothesis

The frozen 256×256 / approximately 200M non-Wan readout route in
`docs/WORLDBRIDGE4D_256_THREE_DATASET_TRAINING_PLAN.md` can be implemented as a
single deterministic three-dataset training executable using BF16 FSDP
FULL_SHARD. It should preserve the exact model/data contract, resume exactly,
and complete a two-rank real-Wan optimizer gate before a four-rank capacity and
formal run.

Falsifiable engineering predictions:

1. Native 21-frame VAE re-encoding produces only `[16,6,32,32]` clean latents;
   old 16×16 latent caches are rejected.
2. The four selected readout layers initialize to `0.30/0.30/0.30/0.10`, and
   geometry adapter plus decoder contain exactly 193,586,693 parameters.
3. Every 20 updates contain 7 Kubric, 6 PointOdyssey, and 7 Dynamic Replica
   updates, with one dataset/prompt shared by all ranks and accumulation
   microsteps in an update.
4. K=6 (or an explicitly frozen K=4 fallback) samples only eligible targets and
   completes at least two real optimizer steps without OOM, non-finite values,
   or distributed reduction failure.
5. Full model/AdamW/counters/per-rank RNG state resumes at the same next update.
