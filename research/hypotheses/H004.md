# H004 — Dense-query 4D latent from feed-forward Wan

## Question

Can a minimally modified pretrained Wan2.1-1.3B map a clean MOVi-F RGB video latent through one DiT forward at rectified-flow time zero into a reusable implicit `Z4D`, such that a lightweight dense `(source,target)` cross-attention decoder predicts a complete source-grid XYZ map for arbitrary source times?

## Prediction

With the Wan VAE frozen and no direct `Z4D` supervision, validity-masked XYZ supervision over balanced diagonal and off-diagonal pairs should (1) strongly overfit fixed pairs from 1–4 clips, (2) decrease for randomly sampled forward, backward, long-gap, and `source>0` queries on the same clips, and (3) complete bounded held-out evaluation with finite pointmap, visible tracking, and occluded-valid tracking metrics. The same `Z4D` must serve every pair and retain Wan's native `[16,6,16,16]` topology.

## Falsification / gates

Do not train until native MOVi-F geometry, Wan's clean-latent shape, timestep-zero convention, and raw velocity sign pass direct audits. The feasibility claim fails if fixed-pair loss does not materially decrease, arbitrary-source loss is flat, gradients do not reach Wan DiT parameters, or the decoder cannot distinguish spatial positions using 2D RoPE.

## Fixed v1 scope

- RGB -> frozen Wan VAE mean latent -> one full Wan DiT forward at flow time zero -> verified negative raw velocity -> `Z4D`.
- Full flattened `Z4D` memory; independent learned source/target embeddings; dense 16x16 query grid; 1–2 cross-attention/FFN blocks with spatial-only 2D RoPE; bilinear 2D upsampler to 128x128 XYZ.
- No GT latent, latent loss, camera input, visibility head, source RGB patch, query self-attention, temporal RoPE, delta-time embedding, or iterative diffusion.
