# H005 — Structured geometry readout from pre-output Wan states

## Hypothesis

Wan2.1's transformer hidden states contain geometry and temporal information that is made unnecessarily difficult to decode by the final rectified-flow normalization and 16-channel RGB-velocity projection. A single exact-time-zero Wan forward that stops at selected transformer blocks, followed by a small geometry adapter, should produce a more decoder-facing reusable latent while preserving the existing dense `(source,target) -> source-grid XYZ pointmap` contract.

The first implementation route fuses zero-based Wan blocks `[5,11,17,23,29]`, projects them to 128 geometry channels, combines a clean-latent local skip, resamples Wan's six latent times to 21 explicit physical-frame planes `Z_dense`, and extracts persistent per-frame `Z_motion` slots. The existing independent `(s,t)` dense queries, cross-attention blocks and bilinear residual pointmap upsampler are retained; only a projected source-local `Z_dense[s]` feature is added to each dense query and structured dense/motion tokens replace the final velocity memory.

The route is falsified at the engineering gate if the Wan `norm_out`/`proj_out` executes, if shapes are not `Z_dense=[B,128,21,16,16]` and `Z_motion=[B,21,M,128]`, if arbitrary batched `(s,t)` queries do not return `[B,K,3,128,128]`, or if masked XYZ gradients cannot reach the geometry adapter/decoder and, in full mode, selected Wan blocks. Scientific quality requires a later matched held-out comparison against H004 velocity readout; single-clip overfit alone is not evidence of superior geometry.

## Evidence

Task `T-20260808163645-fa6db7` implements the first route without changing the public `(s,t) -> pointmap` contract.

- Final unit Run `R-20260808164859-ac2cfe` passed 25 tests. A synthetic Diffusers `WanTransformer3DModel` verified selected hidden shapes `[B,384,D]`, native grid `(6,8,8)`, and zero calls to both `norm_out` and `proj_out`. Structured tests verified dense/motion flattening, arbitrary batched `(s,t)` output `[B,K,3,128,128]`, source-local query injection, adapter/decoder gradients, output-head non-gradients, and `geometry_adapter` freezing semantics.
- Real Wan adapter smoke `R-20260808164446-852e73` produced `Z_dense=[1,128,21,16,16]` and `Z_motion=[1,21,8,128]`, returned diagonal/forward/backward pointmaps, and sent finite gradients to the geometry adapter and decoder. It used 4,795,846 trainable adapter+decoder parameters and 2.80 GiB peak CUDA allocation.
- Frozen-Wan one-clip arbitrary-query overfit `R-20260808164542-1a7038` ran 256 updates. Evaluation normalized loss fell `3.0749 -> 0.1245` (ratio `0.0405`) and EPE fell `16.061 -> 1.011` m; final diagonal/off-diagonal EPE was `0.960/1.032` m and source-zero/source-positive EPE was `1.198/0.930` m. Peak CUDA allocation was 3.13 GiB. This supports engineering trainability, not held-out quality.
- Real full-DiT gradient smoke `R-20260808164726-7f5732` independently confirmed finite gradients in selected Wan parameters, geometry adapter, and decoder while the bypassed output head remained outside the forward route. One update reduced the fixed diagnostic evaluation EPE `15.440 -> 14.671` m with 13.26 GiB peak allocation.

All engineering gates are supported. The stronger claim that the structured hidden-state latent generalizes better than H004's final velocity remains a matched held-out scientific question; layer selection itself was evaluated without using the final 147-clip validation split.

## Layer selection and controlled ablations

The layer-selection protocol used two independent small selection splits and a separate candidate-comparison holdout:

- Split A: train indices `32–47`, probe indices `48–55`.
- Split B: train indices `56–71`, probe indices `72–79`.
- Candidate holdout: train-split indices `80–111`.
- Final validation: all 147 validation clips, indices `0–146`, used only after selection.

The two small splits produced unstable individual mRMR rankings, so standardized probe, correspondence and CKA evidence was aggregated across splits. The aggregate mRMR ranking was `[8,9,13,14,15]`, but its five-layer model was rejected because its two-seed candidate holdout results were not reproducible (seed 2026 improved while seed 2027 degraded). Single layer 13 improved ordinary geometry but degraded late-appearing points on both seeds. Uniform 30-layer entropy gating remained nearly uniform, and seeded top-5 gating improved ordinary geometry while severely degrading late-appearing points. The compact triad `[13,14,15]` was therefore selected before final validation.

For the 1536-step triad candidate, independent candidate holdout indices `80–111` gave the following two-seed mean relative changes against matched original five-layer `[5,11,17,23,29]` baselines: Pointmap `−4.5%`, First-frame `−5.2%`, Arbitrary `−4.4%`, Occluded `−1.3%`, and Late appearing `−16.1%` (negative means lower EPE). This supported promotion to final validation.

The per-seed final validation runs were `R-20260809083901-11ac6b` and `R-20260809083901-674f13`. Effective learned triad weights were approximately `[0.320,0.341,0.339]` for seed 2026 and `[0.316,0.338,0.346]` for seed 2027. The selected model has `4,396,228` trainable geometry-adapter/decoder parameters, compared with approximately `4.80M` for the original structured five-layer route.

### Extended 4096-step budget check

A matched 4096-step two-seed retraining was run after the 1536-step result (`R-20260809084822-3fa51e`, `R-20260809084822-19ce22`). On final 147-clip validation, the two-seed means were Pointmap `3.6157 m`, First-frame `3.6771 m`, Arbitrary `3.6568 m`, and Arbitrary occluded `4.5395 m`, improving over the original five-layer baseline by `15.8%`, `16.9%`, `15.6%`, and `13.1%`, respectively. However, Late appearing was `4.2712 m`, `2.8%` worse than the original baseline and `9.9%` worse than the 1536-step triad mean, driven by seed 2027 (`4.9469 m` versus `3.5955 m` for the 1536-step triad).

Thus the 4096-step run demonstrates a useful overall-geometry/late-appearing trade-off but is not the default final checkpoint for the stated protocol, which prioritizes stable late-appearing and occluded generalization. The final default remains the two-seed 1536-step triad `[13,14,15]`; the 4096-step checkpoints are retained as supplementary budget-sensitivity evidence. No further layer search or longer training is justified without a new, predeclared stopping/selection protocol.
