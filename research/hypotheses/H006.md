# H006 — Motion-slot necessity and capacity

## Hypothesis

The H005 triad's persistent per-frame `Z_motion` slots provide non-local motion information that cannot be fully replaced by its frame-aligned dense planes and source-local query skip. Under the frozen-Wan geometry-adapter protocol, a retrained eight-slot model should therefore reduce held-out arbitrary-source tracking EPE relative to a matched dense-only (`M=0`) model. The slots are not considered necessary if the two-seed mean gain is below 1%, changes sign across seeds, or is confined to the noisy late-appearing subset.

The primary comparison reuses the already trained H005 triad `[13,14,15]`, `M=8` checkpoints and trains matched `M=0` controls with identical 32 training clips, 1,536 updates, pair sampler, decoder seed, and geometry seeds 2026/2027. Evaluation is restricted to the existing train-split candidate holdout indices 80–111 at `pixel_stride=16`; the final 147-clip validation split is not used for architecture selection. Primary metrics are arbitrary all-`(s,t)` and tracking EPE. Pointmap, occluded-valid, temporal-gap, first-frame, and late-appearing EPE are secondary diagnostics.

Two complementary tests distinguish actual slot use from optimization/capacity effects: (1) zero-shot removal or zeroing of the learned slot memory in each trained `M=8` checkpoint, and (2) slot diversity/effective-rank diagnostics across held-out clips. If slots help but are redundant, a smaller matched slot count is an improvement opportunity; if they collapse or weakly affect predictions, explicit pair-conditioned or temporal slot refinement is preferable to increasing slot count. Any follow-up variant is selected only on the same development holdout and must beat `M=8` on both primary metrics without a material occluded/late-appearing regression.
